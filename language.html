Indigenous mother tongues in the Canadian census

What can census tables tell us about Indigenous mother tongues across provinces?

Project walkthrough

DATA 201 · Group project
Exploring Canadian census language patterns using provincial tables.

Rreadxlggplot2Census data
My contribution

I proposed the project topic, completed analysis question three on Indigenous mother tongues, and contributed to the report and presentation introduction.

Process & technical work

Imported provincial Excel sheets into R with readxl, extracted Indigenous mother-tongue counts and “Neither English nor French” counts, converted values to numeric, and visualized provincial comparisons with ggplot2.

Outcome & what I learned

Developed an R workflow for comparing provincial population counts and derived percentages. This project also offers a valuable opportunity to revisit whether a metric answers the intended question.

Limitations & next steps

The report uses first-official-language categories as a retention proxy. This does not directly measure continued use of a mother tongue at home. I would validate the variable definitions and rebuild a retention metric before presenting those percentages as retention rates.

What to look for

I proposed the topic and owned analysis question three. The chart uses first official language spoken as a proxy. A direct measure would compare mother tongue with language spoken at home.
