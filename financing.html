Small-business financing in Canada

How did claim rates and borrowing relative to revenue differ between franchises and non-franchises?

Project walkthrough

DATA 221 · Group project
Comparing franchise and non-franchise businesses using Canada Small Business Financing Program summaries.

PythonpandasMatplotlibExcel
My contribution

I cleaned and combined sheets by business type, developed financial comparison metrics, and used pandas and Matplotlib to analyze and visualize the results.

Process & technical work

Removed irrelevant subcategories, renamed columns, and merged sheets in Excel. In Jupyter, grouped records by business type and calculated claim rate, loan-to-revenue ratio, and net profit margin.

Outcome & what I learned

My report describes higher claim rates and increasing loan reliance among non-franchises. The useful technical outcome was moving from raw sheets to comparable metrics and clear visualizations.

Limitations & next steps

These are aggregate comparisons, not proof that business structure causes default. Before reuse, validate join granularity, metric denominators, and whether loans and claims belong to comparable periods.

What to look for

I chose a line chart to show the loan-to-revenue ratio across 2009–2024 and a bar chart to compare claim rates. The report found higher claim rates for non-franchises and greater reliance on loans in recent years.
