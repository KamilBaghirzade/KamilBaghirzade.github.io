Possible Factors Contributing to Elevated Claim Rates

1.   Concentration of Loans in Ontario and Quebec:

Ontario and Quebec together account for a significant share of all CSBFP loans – Ontario with 36% and Quebec
with 24% (ISED, 2021). This high loan volume raises the likelihood of defaults in absolute numbers.

2.   Economic Vulnerability in Nova Scotia:

Nova Scotia’s economy relies more heavily on seasonal industries like tourism and fisheries, making businesses
more vulnerable to fluctuations. These sectors tend to experience higher volatility, which can negatively affect
loan repayment.

3.   Subsidy Patterns and Financial Behavior:

In Quebec, the government gave out business subsidies that were equal to 100.9% of what it collected in
corporate taxes. In comparison, Ontario and Nova Scotia gave out much less—46.1% and 47.6%, respectively
(Fraser Institute, 2023). This high level of support in Quebec might either encourage businesses to take more
financial risks or be a sign that businesses there are struggling more and need help more often.
