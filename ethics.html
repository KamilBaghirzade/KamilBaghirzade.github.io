Process & technical work

Examined the report’s account of sensitive data moving through shared folders, stakeholder power, organizational responsibility, and trade-offs between access, oversight, and privacy.

Outcome & what I learned

The report recommends tighter role-based access, stronger monitoring, retention limits, and continuing support for affected people. It connects technical data handling to organizational responsibilities.

Limitations & next steps
